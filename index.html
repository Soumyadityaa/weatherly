About Weatherly

Real-time global weather intelligence built with open meteorological standards

High-Resolution Forecast Models

Weatherly aggregates global and regional numerical weather prediction (NWP) models via Open-Meteo, providing hourly and 7-day forecasts with automatic local timezone synchronization.

WMO Standardized Codes

All atmospheric conditions follow official World Meteorological Organization (WMO) weather interpretation codes, paired with day/night solar calculations and custom vector illustrations.

Privacy-First Preferences

Your preferred temperature scale, wind speed unit, visual theme, and recent city searches are stored strictly in your browser's LocalStorage. Geolocation is only accessed upon explicit request.
